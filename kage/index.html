KAGE

Tap to leap. Climb forever.

KAGE is a one-finger climb between two endless walls. Your ninja climbs on his own; tap and he leaps to the other wall. Fly through ensō rings to become a shadow, dodge thrown stars, outrun crumbling stone, and take on three new challenges every day.

Free on iPhone and Android, with optional rewarded ads and cosmetic skins.
