KAGE

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 11, 2026

KAGE is a game made by Northpixel ("we"). This policy explains what information is used when you play KAGE on iPhone, iPad or Android, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive any information about you.

Your game progress (best height, shadows, skins, settings and daily challenges) is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

KAGE offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad to continue a run or to earn shadows. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and the approximate location it indicates), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the app asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with PRIVACY SETTINGS on the game's start screen.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Purchases (exclusive skins, the skin bundle and No Ads) are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the store only tells the app which items you own.

Children

KAGE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information held by Google for advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
