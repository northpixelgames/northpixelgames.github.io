Common questions

I bought a skin or No Ads, but it's gone

Open the skin shop and tap RESTORE (top right). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Purchases also come back on their own when you install KAGE on a new phone.

An ad didn't give me my reward

The reward is given only when an ad is watched to the end. If no ad is available (for example with no internet connection), the game says "No ad ready right now"; try again in a moment.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In Europe, tap PRIVACY SETTINGS on the start screen. On iPhone you can also change tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Your progress (best height, shadows, skins) is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. Bought items come back with RESTORE.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or in your Google Play order history (Android).
